About

About SeatBreak: escape rooms you solve from your chair

SeatBreak is a free seated VR escape-room game that runs in the Meta Quest browser, with no install. It was made by Zach Wennstedt at madwackd.ai studio.

Play now How to play

What is SeatBreak?

SeatBreak is a set of twelve escape rooms you solve without standing up. You can play with bare hands, controllers or gaze, in chair, recliner or tabletop mode. It also plays on a normal screen. Eleven rooms are open and The Neon Road is coming soon.

Each room is a chain of clues. After you escape, you see the real science or history behind what you just used, such as levers, light, buoyancy, optical illusions and stage magic.

Who is SeatBreak for?

Families, kids, teens and adults who want a puzzle game they can play sitting down, including people who cannot or do not want to stand or walk in VR. Rooms start at ages 10 and up and rise to a timed Expert vault. The play guide lists every room with its age range.

Who made it?

Zach Wennstedt made SeatBreak at madwackd.ai studio. All art, music and sound are original, and Bolt, your guide drone, speaks with recorded family voices.

The aim is a game that is fun first, with learning built in. Read more on the mission page.

Does it track me?

No. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Progress is stored only on your own device. The privacy page has the details.
