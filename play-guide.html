How to play

How to play SeatBreak on Meta Quest, seated and hands-free

SeatBreak runs in the Meta Quest Browser with nothing to install. Open the site, pick a seating mode and press the VR button. You can play with bare hands, controllers or gaze, and it also works on a normal screen.

Play now About SeatBreak

How do I start in three steps?

  1. Open the browser. Put on your Meta Quest and open the Meta Quest Browser, then go to www.seatbreak.com.
  2. Pick how you sit. Choose Chair, Recliner or Table (a mini room on your table).
  3. Press the VR button. Press "Open on Quest to play in VR". No headset? Press "Play on this screen" instead.

You begin in the Lobby, a night-time village square. Look at a glowing building for a moment to step inside its room.

What rooms can I play?

There are twelve rooms, from easy rooms for young players up to a timed Expert vault. The Neon Road is still being built.

  • Toy Workshop (Easy, ages 10+): Find three missing shapes to unlock the toy door, with shapes and counting along the way.
  • The Fun House (Easy, ages 10+): A carnival room where you ring, drum and splat your way to the stuck exit lever. Teaches rhythm and colour mixing.
  • Star Observatory (Medium, ages 10+): Bring the lights back on, then read the planets and stars to find the exit code.
  • The Mind Bender (Medium, ages 10+): An illusion gallery with two paintings you can step into: a pencil-drawing world and an op-art colour tunnel.
  • The Goo Lab (Medium, ages 10+): Squish, mould, mix and stretch slime and colour to unstick the exit hatch.
  • The Sunken Lab (Medium, ages 10+): Escape a filling test tank on a raft before your 15 minutes of air run out. Buoyancy and measuring.
  • Clockwork Library (Hard, ages 13+): A logic chain of gears, bells, clocks and a safe in a locked library.
  • The Grand Illusion (Hard, ages 12+): Chained to a chair with three padlocks, you solve riddles to open each lock and gain more slack.
  • The Laser Attic (Hard, ages 12+): Use light and mirrors in an inventor's attic to find the way out through the roof hatch.
  • Zero Hour Vault (Expert, timed): A 20-minute timed vault: restore power, crack the codeword and open the vault.
  • The Magic House (Ages 10+): Four illusionist ranks, from Apprentice to Grand Illusionist, built on real stage-magic principles.
  • The Neon Road (Ages 10+): A recliner ride through a neon world with visual-sense challenges. Coming soon.

The Lobby also has a Star Catch mini-game, hidden secrets to find and a Super Genius trophy. Bolt, your guide drone, gives hints in recorded family voices.

What are the controls?

  • Hands: poke things with a finger, pinch to grab, and point at the floor and pinch to glide.
  • Lean: lean your body to glide while seated.
  • Gaze: look at anything for a moment to use it. No hands or controllers are needed.
  • Controllers: flick the thumbstick to turn in small steps.
  • On a screen: drag to look, click to use things, W A S D to move, Q / E to turn, H for a hint, M for more and T to ask Bolt.

Questions players ask

How do I play SeatBreak on Meta Quest?

Open the Meta Quest Browser, go to www.seatbreak.com, pick Chair, Recliner or Table mode, then press "Open on Quest to play in VR". There is nothing to install and no account to make.

Do I need controllers to play?

No. You can play with bare hands (poke things with a finger and pinch to grab), with controllers, or by gaze: look at something for a moment to use it.

How do I move and turn while seated?

Lean to glide, or point at the floor and pinch. In Chair mode you can also look at the floor rings to glide. With controllers, a flick of the thumbstick turns you in small steps. Nothing requires walking or standing.

Is SeatBreak free?

Yes. SeatBreak is free to play today, with no ads, no accounts and no purchases in the game.

What ages is SeatBreak for?

The rooms run from ages 10 and up (Toy Workshop) through ages 13 and up (Clockwork Library), plus a timed Expert vault. Young children should play with a grown-up nearby, and the Quest headset has its own age rules, so please follow the maker's guidance.

Does SeatBreak collect my data?

No. There are no ads, analytics or trackers, and no account. Your progress is saved only on your own device and is erased if you clear your browser data. The only message we receive is one you choose to send through the contact page.

How do I leave a room?

In VR, look down at the ring on your chest to open the menu, choose More, then Lobby. On a screen, press M for More. You can also ask Bolt, your guide drone, to take you to the lobby.

What if I feel dizzy?

Stop, take the headset off and rest until you feel well. Take a break at least every 30 minutes and keep the area around you clear. SeatBreak has no strobing or flashing and is played seated, but VR is not for everyone. The op-art tunnel has a plain Calm Corner and a switch to soften its patterns.

Can I play without a VR headset?

Yes. Open www.seatbreak.com on a normal screen and press "Play on this screen". Drag to look around, click to use things, use W A S D to move and Q / E to turn. On a touch screen, drag to look and tap things to use them.
