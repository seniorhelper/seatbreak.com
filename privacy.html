Privacy

Short and plain: we collect nothing about you while you play.

Last updated November 21, 2026.

The game

  • SeatBreak runs entirely in your browser. It makes no requests to any other site while you play, and it has no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, age, email or location.
  • Your progress (stars, best times, skin choice, settings and secrets found) is saved only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data erases it.
  • The Ask Bolt box is answered on your device by a built-in guide. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. If you use the microphone button, your browser's own speech feature may process your voice under your browser vendor's rules. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice.
  • Room Scan and Table mode use your headset's room tools only after your headset asks for permission. The scan stays on your headset and we never receive it.
  • There are no purchases in SeatBreak today. If paid skins are added later, they will go through the platform's own store, and this page will be updated first.

Children

SeatBreak has rooms for ages 5 and up, and we collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side. Young children should play with a grown-up nearby, and the Quest headset has its own age rules, so please follow the maker's guidance.

The contact form

If you write to us through the contact page, we receive the name, email, role and message you type. We use them only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share them. To have your message deleted, email us and ask.

Play comfort and safety

Take a break at least every 30 minutes, stop if you feel dizzy or unwell, and keep the area around you clear. SeatBreak has no strobing or flashing, but VR is not for everyone.
